Roberts Road Complex

Logistics center of SpaceX Cape Operations. Also the hub for Starship Cape operations.

Starbase

Starbase • Active

Production Site

Production and Final Stacking of Starship vehicles. Also stores transport stands, as well as construction of infrastructure.
Starbase • ACTIVE

Masseys

Main pre-launch testing site. All Cryo testing, as well as Ship static fire testing, is conducted here. Has a variety of stands for different purposes, also usually stores mobile test stands.
Starbase • Under Construction

Pad A/1

Original launch site for Intigrated Test Flights. Had many quirks, most of which are gone now aside from the tower itself. Currently undergoing rework to support V3.
Starbase • Active

Pad B/2

Second launch site for full Starships. Constructed while Pad A/1 supported first flights. Had major reworks compared to first pad due to lessons learned. Currently the only active Starship pad.
